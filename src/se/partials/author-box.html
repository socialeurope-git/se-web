AUTHOR PROFILE

Alaa Alkhatib

Alaa Alkhatib is a Vienna-based Austrian-Syrian researcher and policy analyst with more than 25 years of research experience, including previous work at IIASA and the IAEA. Since 2016, his independent research has focused increasingly on Syria and the wider Middle East, political Islam, migration, identity and disinformation. He publishes through InfoSalam, an independent multilingual platform in Arabic, English and German.
